Cosplay is my heart, storytelling is my soul

I'm Frences, a cosplayer, model, and creative based in Ilocos Norte, Philippines. For the past three years, what began as a quiet passion for dressing up and doing my own makeup in my room has grown into a full-fledged creative path.

I've had the honor of judging cosplay competitions and leading our local cosplay organization here in Ilocos Norte, helping nurture new talent in our community. My work has been featured in regional conventions and collaborative shoots with local photographers and brands.

Moving forward, I'm looking to collaborate on editorial projects, brand campaigns, and convention appearances that celebrate creativity, storytelling, and Filipino talent in cosplay and modeling.
