About Me

7+ Years of
Cosplay

Cosplay is my heart, anime is my soul

Hi, I'm Jen! I'm an anime cosplayer who breathes life into the characters I love. From meticulously hand-crafting armor pieces to styling the perfect wig, every costume is a journey into the worlds that shaped me.

My adventure began at my first anime convention, surrounded by fans who shared the same passion. Since then, I've cosplayed over 80 characters, won awards at major conventions, and built friendships with amazing creators across the globe.
